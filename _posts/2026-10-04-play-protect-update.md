# ⚠️ Important Compatibility Warning

This method **might not work on your device**.

Samsung and Google regularly change the Android Setup Wizard, accessibility features, Play Protect, and other system components through firmware updates.

Therefore:

- ❌ The Camera/QR scanner shortcut may not work.
- ❌ **Legal Help** may not be available.
- ❌ You may see different menus.
- ❌ Some options may have been removed or patched.
- ❌ The procedure may behave differently on newer firmware.
- ❌ Steps that work on one Samsung model may not work on another.

If something doesn't match what you see on your device, **do not assume you did something wrong**. Your firmware may simply be different.

# 📷 Step 1 — Open the Camera Scanner

On the **first setup screen**, tap the **blank white area near the bottom of the screen approximately 5–10 times**.

If your firmware supports this shortcut, the **Camera QR Scanner** should appear.

### Scan this QR code:

After scanning the QR code, you should be redirected to the **Wi-Fi connectivity** screen.

> 💡 **Tip:** The exact behavior of the QR scanner can vary between Samsung models and firmware versions.

---

# 📶 Step 2 — Connect to Wi-Fi

Once you reach the Wi-Fi screen:

1. Select your Wi-Fi network.
2. Enter the Wi-Fi password.
3. Tap **OK**.
4. Press the **Back** button repeatedly until you return to the screen where you started.

You should now remain connected to Wi-Fi.

---

# ♿ Step 3 — Open Accessibility

Next:

1. Open **Accessibility**.
2. Scroll down.
3. Open **Switch Access**.
4. Tap **Skip** if prompted.

---

# 🔊 Step 4 — Configure Google Text-to-Speech

Inside **Universal Switch**:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Scroll down.
3. Turn on **Voice Feedback**.
4. Open **Text-to-Speech**.
5. Change the preferred speech engine to **Google Text-to-Speech**.

---

# ⚙️ Step 5 — Open the Google TTS Settings

Now:

1. Open the **Google Text-to-Speech settings**.
2. Tap **Feedback**.
3. Look for the clickable **Legal Help** option.

You can use the following image as a reference:

**Reference image:**  
https://ibb.co/BH2GfVmM

> ⚠️ **Important:** If you don't see **Legal Help**, your device may be running newer firmware where this particular route has been removed or patched.
>
> **This method might not work on newer devices.**

---

# ▶️ Step 6 — Open YouTube and Chrome

If the Legal Help option is available:

1. Tap **Legal Help**.
2. A help page containing a video should appear.
3. Open the video with **YouTube**.
4. From YouTube, open **Chrome**.
5. When Chrome asks you to sign in, select **Use without an account**.

---

# 🛡️ Step 7 — Open Google Play Services

In Chrome:

1. Search for **Google Play Services**.
2. Open the first **Google Play Store** result.
3. The Google Play Store should open.

> ⚠️ **Your search results may look different depending on your device, region, and Google account configuration.**

---

# 🛡️ Step 8 — Play Protect Settings

In the Play Store:

1. Tap your **profile icon** in the top-right corner.
2. Open **Play Protect**.
3. Tap the **Settings** icon.
4. Review the available Play Protect settings.

If you are performing authorized testing on your own device and need Play Protect temporarily disabled, use the available Play Protect controls shown on your firmware.

> **Note:** Google may change these settings or restrict access to them in future Play Store/Android updates.
