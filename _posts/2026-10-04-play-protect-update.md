## What is this?

If you're following the Samsung Knox guide [Yash Garg](https://yashgarg.dev/posts/bypass-knox-samsung/)
, you may run into **Google Play Protect blocking applications** required later in the process.

This post covers the additional steps needed to get past that issue on some Samsung firmware versions.

> **Important:** This method is firmware-dependent and **might not work on your device**. Some options may be missing or different on newer versions.

---

# 📷 Step 1 — Open the Camera Scanner

On the **first setup screen**, tap the **white/blank area near the bottom of the screen 5–10 times**.

The Camera QR scanner should appear.

Once the scanner appears, scan the QR code below.

<p align="center">
  <img src="/images/play-protect-qr.png"
       width="300"
       alt="Play Protect QR Code">
</p>

After scanning the QR code, you will be directed to the **Wi-Fi connectivity** screen.

> **Note:** If the Camera scanner doesn't appear, your firmware may not support this method.

---

# 📶 Step 2 — Connect to Wi-Fi

1. Select your Wi-Fi network.
2. Enter your Wi-Fi password.
3. Click **OK**.
4. Press the **Back** button repeatedly until you reach the start screen from where you started.

You should now be connected to Wi-Fi.

---

# ♿ Step 3 — Open Accessibility

1. Open **Accessibility**.
2. Scroll down.
3. Click **Switch Access**.
4. Click **Skip**.

---

# 🔊 Step 4 — Configure Universal Switch

1. Open **Universal Switch**.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Scroll down.
4. Turn on **Voice Feedback**.
5. Click **Text-to-Speech**.
6. Change the preferred speech engine to **Google Text-to-Speech**.

---

# ⚙️ Step 5 — Open Google Text-to-Speech Settings

1. Click the **Settings** option for Google Text-to-Speech.
2. Click **Feedback**.
3. Look for the clickable **Legal Help** option.

### Reference Image

https://ibb.co/BH2GfVmM

> **⚠️ If Legal Help is not shown:** your device may be too new, and this method may no longer work on your firmware.

---

# ▶️ Step 6 — Open YouTube

1. Click **Legal Help**.
2. A page containing a video should appear.
3. Click **Open with YouTube**.
4. YouTube will open.
5. Open **Chrome**.
6. When Chrome asks you to sign in, select **Use without an account**.

---

# 🌐 Step 7 — Search for Google Play Services

In Chrome:

1. Click the search bar.
2. Type:

```text
Google Play Services
```

3. Click the **first Play Store option**.

The Google Play Store should now open.

---

# 🛡️ Step 8 — Disable Play Protect

In the Play Store:

1. Click the **top-right profile/menu icon**.
2. Click **Play Protect**.
3. Open **Settings**.
4. Turn **both Play Protect options OFF**.

You're done.

> **Note:** The Play Store interface can differ between Android versions. If the options aren't available, your firmware may have changed or patched this method.
