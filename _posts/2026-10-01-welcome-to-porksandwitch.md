Welcome to **PorkSandwitch** 👋

This is my little corner of the internet where I post things I find interesting.

That could be pretty much anything.

## What you'll find here

I'll be sharing things like:

- 💻 Technology and software
- 📥 Interesting downloads
- 🌐 Cool websites and internet discoveries
- 🛠️ Useful tools
- 🎮 Games when I find something worth talking about
- 💭 Random thoughts and things I want to say
- 🔎 Interesting stuff I discover while browsing the internet

Basically, if I find something cool enough to share, it might end up here.

## Why I made this

There is a ridiculous amount of interesting stuff on the internet.

Sometimes I find a useful program.

Sometimes I discover a website I've never heard of.

Sometimes I find a project, a tool, a download, or just something weird and interesting.

Instead of letting all of that disappear into my browser history, I want to keep some of it here.

## No particular rules

This isn't going to be a serious technology publication or a gaming news site.

It's just **my website**.

I'll write about whatever I feel like writing about.

Some posts might be useful.

Some might be completely random.

And that's kind of the point.

Thanks for stopping by.

**Have a look around. You might find something interesting.** 👋
