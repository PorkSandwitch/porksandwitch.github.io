# 🎥 YouTube Video Enhancer

**Make YouTube videos look sharper, cleaner, and more defined. ✨**

I made **YouTube Video Enhancer** as a simple and lightweight way to improve the visual appearance of YouTube videos without installing a heavy browser extension.

## ✨ What does it do?

It applies lightweight visual adjustments to the video, giving you control over how strong the enhancement should be.

### 🎚️ Choose your level

**🌿 Natural**  
A subtle improvement for everyday watching.

**✨ Clear**  
A stronger boost in clarity and definition.

**🔥 Strong**  
Maximum enhancement when you want the effect pushed further.

You can also enable **🤖 Auto Enhance**, which automatically chooses an appropriate enhancement level based on the video's resolution.

## 🎛️ Simple controls

Everything is controlled through a small, draggable panel that stays out of your way.

Your settings are remembered too, so you don't have to configure everything again every time you open YouTube. 💾

## ⚡ Lightweight by design

The goal was simple: **make videos look better without making the experience complicated.**

No unnecessary features.  
No bloated interface.  
Just install → choose your level → watch. ▶️

## 🚀 Get it

Ready to try it?

**[👉 View YouTube Video Enhancer on GitHub](https://github.com/PorkSandwitch/YouTube-Video-Enhancer)**

It works with popular userscript managers including:

- 🐒 Tampermonkey
- 🐒 Violentmonkey

## 📌 One important thing

YouTube Video Enhancer **doesn't increase the actual resolution** of a video.

A 720p video won't magically become 1080p. 😄

Instead, it enhances the way the existing video is displayed to give it a sharper and cleaner appearance.

## 💡 Inspiration

This project was inspired by **[youtube-sharpness-enhancer](https://github.com/Kirchlive/youtube-sharpness-enhancer)**.

I took the idea further and built my own lightweight enhancer with adjustable enhancement levels, automatic enhancement, and a simple control panel.
